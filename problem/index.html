REAL, APPARENT, AUTHORIZED

A balance is not a diagnosis.

Real losses

Physical leakage needs appropriate field evidence. An anomaly alone cannot establish its cause.

Apparent losses

Metering and billing issues can affect the balance without being a physical leak.

Unbilled authorized use

Authorized use can be non-revenue water without being either a billing error or a leak.

EXISTING ALTERNATIVES

The work is not greenfield.

FIDO, Xylem, acoustic logger vendors, hydraulic-modeling consultants, AWWA-style audits and crew knowledge already address parts of this problem. LeakEvidence’s advantage remains a hypothesis to test locally.
