Vision

Utility teams reconcile water balances and field evidence before allocating repair crews.

Mission

Help NRW managers and distribution operations directors move from fragmented evidence to a defensible human-approved decision, with provenance and explicit uncertainty.

WHERE WE ARE

Research before scale.

Customer demand, accepted pricing, production integrations and local detection performance remain unproven. No team biography, funding, incorporation, address, trademark or domain-ownership claim is made here.

Expansion into AMI analytics or capital replacement prioritization is conditional on field outcomes. Software opportunity must be sized from verified buyer counts, tested contract value and reachable share, accounting for service and integration costs—not water-loss volume or equipment capex.
